Prescott is a gift for portraits. Within about twenty minutes of downtown you get granite boulders, four lakes, ponderosa pine forest and a brick downtown that looks good in every season. Here are the spots I come back to most, what each one looks like on camera, and when to go.

If you're planning a session, this is a good place to start. If you'd rather leave it to me, that's what the planning call is for.

## Watson Lake

The Granite Dells are the most recognizable backdrop in Prescott: rounded granite stacked above still water. It photographs as warm, textured and a little otherworldly.

- **Best light:** sunrise for glassy water and very few people; the last hour before sunset for warm, glowing rock.
- **Good for:** maternity, couples, creative portraits and anyone who wants "this is clearly Arizona."
- **Plan for:** uneven rock. Wear shoes you can scramble in and bring a change for the photos.

## Willow Lake

Watson's quieter neighbor. Willow Lake has more open shoreline and long views of the Dells across the water, so it reads softer and more spacious than Watson.

- **Best light:** sunset. The open sky turns the whole scene gold.
- **Good for:** flowing dresses, couples walking, family sessions where kids need room to run.
- **Plan for:** the water level changes through the year, which changes the shoreline. I check it before we go.

## Thumb Butte

Pine forest a few minutes west of downtown, with big views over town from the trail. It's the easy choice when you want shade, greenery and a mountain feel without a long drive.

- **Best light:** late afternoon, when the sun filters through the pines.
- **Good for:** autumn and themed shoots, headshots with a natural backdrop, anyone who doesn't want a desert look.
- **Plan for:** a short walk from the parking area, and a busy trailhead on weekend mornings.

## Goldwater Lake

A small, quiet lake ringed by pines just south of town. The wooden dock over the water is the star: it gives you a clean line to stand on, open sky behind you and reflections at your feet.

![Woman standing on a wooden dock at sunset at Goldwater Lake in Prescott, Arizona](/photos/portraits/MJR05118.jpg "Sunset on the dock at Goldwater Lake.")

- **Best light:** sunset, when the sky over the water does most of the work.
- **Good for:** solo portraits, maternity, couples and anyone who wants water without a crowd.
- **Plan for:** a dock that's popular with people fishing. Weekday evenings are the calmest.

## Courthouse Square and Whiskey Row

Downtown Prescott gives you a classic small-town look: the courthouse lawn under big elm trees, brick storefronts and the historic bars along Whiskey Row.

- **Best light:** early morning before the square fills up, or the hour before sunset when the buildings shade the street.
- **Good for:** headshots and personal branding, engagement photos, anyone who wants an urban look with character.
- **Plan for:** crowds on weekends and during events. Fall color and the holiday lights are worth timing for.

## Lynx Lake

Tall ponderosa pines around a small lake, southeast of town. It stays green and noticeably cooler in summer, which makes it my go-to from June through August.

- **Best light:** morning, or late afternoon once the trees soften the sun.
- **Good for:** summer sessions, families, maternity in a forest setting.
- **Plan for:** a short drive out of town and a popular spot on summer weekends.

## Timing your session in Prescott

Prescott sits around 5,400 feet, so the light and weather behave a little differently from the Valley.

- **Golden hour is the goal.** I schedule most outdoor sessions for the last hour or so before sunset. The exact time moves a lot between June and December, and I'll send it with your confirmation.
- **Fall is the favorite.** From October into early November, the elms downtown and the trees around the lakes turn color, and the temperatures are perfect.
- **Monsoon season brings drama.** July through September often means afternoon storms. They also bring the best skies of the year, so we keep a backup date and watch the forecast together.
- **Winter is underrated.** Fewer people, low warm light all day, and the occasional snow day.
